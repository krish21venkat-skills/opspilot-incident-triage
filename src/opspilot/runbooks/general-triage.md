# General triage when the cause is unclear

## When to use
The alarm fired but the logs hold no clear signal, or the resource is not one OpsPilot manages.

## Steps
1. Notify the on-call engineer with the alarm name, state reason and time.
2. Record what evidence was and was not available.
3. Do not propose any change to infrastructure.
